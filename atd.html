ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is a premier private engineering institution located in a lush 37-acre green campus in Anad, Nedumangad, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. Established in 2002 by the V.N. Gangadhara Panicker Memorial (VNGP) Trust, the college holds a prestigious NAAC-A Grade Accreditation

Academic Programs & Intake

MCET offers multiple undergraduate and postgraduate courses spanning Engineering, Management, and IT fields. The primary programs include:

B.Tech Streams: Computer Science & Engineering (120 seats), AI & Machine Learning (60 seats), Biotechnology & Biochemical Engineering (60 seats), Civil Engineering, Electrical & Electronics, Electronics & Communication, Electronics & Computer, and Mechanical Engineering.
Postgraduate & Management: Formally affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).
Leadership: Guided by Director Dr. Ashalatha Thampuran and Principal Dr. S. Sheela.

Affiliation & Governance

Approvals: Fully approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and accredited by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO).
University Affiliation: Master of Computer Applications (MCA), Master of Technology (M.Tech) in thrust areas like Power Systems, and management degrees (BBA Honours & MBA) managed under the Mohandas Institute of Management (MIM).

Industry Tie-ups & Innovation

MoUs & Collaborations: The Computer Science and AI/ML divisions maintain active partnerships with tech firms like Alibi Global Private Limited and Netics IT Innovations to provide hands-on experience in emerging software tech.
Entrepreneurship: The campus houses the Gravity-IEDC (Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Cell) to help students incubate startups, develop technical prototypes, and work on digital solutions.
